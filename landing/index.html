For the person training

Built for a room with bad signal and no spare hands.

◉

Live workout logging

Sessions run over Socket.io, so a set logged on the floor shows up for the trainer as it happens — no refresh, no end-of-day upload.

⇅

Offline-first sync

Basements kill signal. Every set is queued locally and replayed automatically when the connection returns, so a workout is never lost to a dead bar of reception.

⏱

Between-set rest timer

Starts on the set you just logged and counts down where your thumb already is, instead of in a second app.

★

Automatic PR detection

Estimated 1RM and volume records are recognised the moment you log them, and surfaced as a banner — you find out you hit a personal best without doing arithmetic.

▦

Reusable templates

Save any completed session as a template and start next week's version pre-filled.

◪

Progress charts

Volume and strength trends per lift, so progress is visible over months rather than remembered set by set.

◈

Guided intake & lifestyle

A structured onboarding captures goals, history and constraints up front, plus ongoing lifestyle check-ins the coach can actually act on.

⚑

Opt-in weekly leaderboard

A friendly ranking within your own trainer's cohort — off by default. Nobody appears in it without choosing to.

Over months, not sets

Progress you don't have to remember.

Every logged set feeds volume and estimated-1RM trends per lift. Personal records are detected as they happen rather than worked out afterwards, so the app tells you the session mattered while you are still in the gym.

  • Per-lift charts — strength and volume trends drawn from your own logged sets.
  • Automatic PR detection — estimated 1RM and volume records, flagged the moment they land.
  • Templates — turn a session that worked into next week's starting point.

For the person coaching

See who is actually turning up.

The trainer dashboard leads with a seven-day compliance chip per client — red, amber or green — so a roster of thirty tells you where to spend your attention before you have opened a single profile.

  • 7-day RAG compliance chip — a red/amber/green indicator of sessions completed against sessions planned. A traffic light, not a prediction.
  • Client management — roster, per-client overview, and history in one place.
  • Workout & program builder — build single sessions or multi-week programmes and assign them.
  • Gym TV mode — a display view for a screen on the gym wall.
